# ocelot icon designs

Three design directions for the ocelot app icon. Each section contains a
ready-to-use prompt for fal.ai Nano Banana Pro (or any major image
generator) plus color palette and rationale.

## Design 1: The Spotted Sentinel (realistic portrait)

**Concept:** A close-up, 3/4 view ocelot face. Golden-amber base fur with
the signature rosette pattern (dark spots with light centers) wrapping
around the cheeks and forehead. Piercing pale-green eyes. White fur below
the muzzle, dark stripes from the eyes to the ears.

**Palette:**
- Fur: `#D4A555` -> `#8B5A2B`
- Spots: `#2A1810`
- Eyes: `#A8D080`
- Background: `#0A1929` -> `#1A2942`

**Prompt:**

```
App icon, 1024x1024 square, photorealistic close-up portrait of an
ocelot's face in three-quarter view, looking directly at the viewer.
Golden-amber fur with distinctive rosette spots - dark rings with
lighter centers - across the cheeks and forehead. White fur on the
muzzle and chin. Two dark stripes running from each eye to the ears.
Piercing pale-green eyes with vertical pupils, sharp focus on the eyes.
Subtle film grain. Dark background gradient from deep teal #0A1929 to
midnight blue #1A2942. Soft rim light on the fur edge. Style of Apple
marketing render, premium tech aesthetic. Centered, generous padding
around the face, suitable for an app icon mask.
```

## Design 2: The Geometric Glyph (minimalist mark)

**Concept:** A stylized "O" formed entirely from ocelot features. Two
pointed ears (with white spots on the back) form the top. Two glowing
green eyes sit where the "O" curves. The tail wraps around the bottom
to complete the circle. Single-color linework on a flat color field.

**Palette:**
- Background: `#0F1419`
- Linework: `#F5F0E8`
- Eye accent: `#7FE89C`
- Warm accent: `#E89A4D`

**Prompt:**

```
App icon, 1024x1024 square, minimalist geometric mark. A stylized
capital letter "O" formed from ocelot features: two pointed cat ears
with small white spots on the back form the top of the O, two glowing
green eyes sit in the upper portion where the O curves, and an ocelot
tail with ring markings wraps around the bottom to complete the
circle. Single-color linework in warm off-white #F5F0E8 on a flat
near-black background #0F1419. The eyes glow soft mint green #7FE89C.
Bold geometric strokes, Bauhaus-influenced flat vector illustration,
generous padding around the mark, perfectly centered, suitable for a
macOS app icon mask. No gradients, no realistic textures.
```

## Design 3: The Quantum Ocelot (tech / sci-fi)

**Concept:** A side-profile ocelot silhouette, but the body is filled
with gold-and-teal circuit traces, glowing nodes, and constellation
dots. The eye is a single bright pulsing point of light.

**Palette:**
- Background: `#050810`
- Silhouette outline: `#1A3A4F`
- Circuit traces: `#D4A555`
- Glowing nodes: `#7FE89C` and `#FF8C42`
- Eye: `#FFFFFF` core with `#7FE89C` halo

**Prompt:**

```
App icon, 1024x1024 square, side-profile silhouette of an ocelot in
mid-stride, but the interior of the silhouette is filled with intricate
golden-amber circuit traces, glowing teal and orange nodes, and small
constellation-like dots, like a neural network made physical. The
single visible eye is a bright pulsing white point with a soft
mint-green halo. The ocelot's outline is dark teal, the only "real"
element. Deep void-black background #050810 with very subtle
horizontal scan lines. Cyberpunk-meets-natural-history aesthetic, Apple
visionOS color palette, high contrast, glowing accents, technical
schematic feel. Centered with generous padding, suitable for app icon
mask.
```
